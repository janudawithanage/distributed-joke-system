A look behind the laughs

A distributed system with a simple purpose: get good jokes from people to people. Every submission is reviewed before it joins the collection.

01. Write & submit

The Submit Service sends your joke to a durable RabbitMQ queue.

02. A human touch

The Moderate Service lets a reviewer edit, approve, or reject each submission.

03. Into the collection

The ETL Service saves approved jokes to MySQL or MongoDB and shares category updates.

04. A fresh laugh

The Joke Service serves the collection through Kong, our API gateway.
